\section{Problem}
\label{s:problem}

We are given a set of potential facility locations $F$ and a set of customers
$C$. Each facility $f \in F$ has an opening cost $o_f$ and a capacity $q_f$.
Each customer $c \in C$ has demand $d_c$ and a service cost $a_{c,f}$ for
being served from facility $f$. Vehicles are identical: each pays a fixed
usage cost $u$ and may drive at most $T$ total round-trip distance, where
$\delta_{c,f}$ is the round-trip distance between $c$ and $f$. Up to
$V = |C|$ vehicles may be used per facility (in the worst case, one per
customer). The goal is a \emph{lower bound} on the total cost of opening
facilities, allocating customers, and renting vehicles, computed quickly
through the LP relaxation.

\section{LP Model}
\label{s:model}

\paragraph{Decision variables.} All continuous (this is the LP relaxation of
the natural mixed-integer program):
\begin{itemize}
  \item $y_f \in [0,1]$ for each $f \in F$: fraction of facility $f$ opened.
  \item $x_{c,f} \in [0,1]$ for each $c \in C, f \in F$: fraction of customer
        $c$'s demand served from facility $f$.
  \item $v_f \in [0, V]$ for each $f \in F$: continuous number of vehicles
        deployed at facility $f$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Objective.} Minimize
\[
  \sum_{f \in F} o_f\, y_f
  \;+\; \sum_{c \in C}\sum_{f \in F} a_{c,f}\, x_{c,f}
  \;+\; u \sum_{f \in F} v_f .
\]

\paragraph{Constraints.}
\begin{align}
  \sum_{f \in F} x_{c,f} &= 1
    && \forall c \in C
    \tag{full service} \\
  x_{c,f} &\le y_f
    && \forall c \in C,\, f \in F
    \tag{open before serve} \\
  \sum_{c \in C} d_c\, x_{c,f} &\le q_f\, y_f
    && \forall f \in F
    \tag{facility capacity} \\
  \sum_{c \in C} \delta_{c,f}\, x_{c,f} &\le T\, v_f
    && \forall f \in F
    \tag{vehicle workload} \\
  v_f &\le V\, y_f
    && \forall f \in F
    \tag{vehicle requires open}
\end{align}

The vehicle-workload constraint is the standard LP-relaxation surrogate for
``each vehicle drives at most $T$'': summed over the $v_f$ vehicles at the
facility, total round-trip distance cannot exceed $T \cdot v_f$. Because
$v_f$ is continuous in the relaxation, fractional vehicle counts are
allowed.

\section{Implementation}
\label{s:impl}

We use Google OR-Tools'
\texttt{ortools.linear\_solver.python.model\_builder} API with the SCIP
backend that the stencil already constructs; since every variable is
continuous, SCIP solves the model as a pure linear program. We did not
switch to the original CP solver (\texttt{pywrapcp}) because it only
supports integer variables and cannot express the continuous relaxation
that the lower bound requires.

The implementation lives entirely in \texttt{src/lpinstance.py}. The
\texttt{solve()} method builds the variables and constraints above using
\texttt{model.new\_num\_var}, \texttt{model.add}, and
\texttt{model.minimize}, then reports $\lceil \text{obj} \rceil$ as the
integer \texttt{Result} field required by the grader.

\paragraph{Verifier.} A standalone script \texttt{src/verify.py}
recomputes every constraint on the fractional solution returned by SCIP
(absolute tolerance $10^{-6}$) and independently recomputes the
objective from $y, x, v$ to confirm it matches
\texttt{solver.objective\_value}. It is run manually on a handful of
instances as a sanity check; it is \emph{not} wired into
\texttt{run.sh} or \texttt{runAll.sh}, so grading-time output is
unaffected.
